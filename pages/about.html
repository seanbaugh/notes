About Sean

Sean Baugh is a professional musician and conductor with a deep love for choral music and the human voice. His work spans the podium, the rehearsal room, and — increasingly — the page, where he writes about the craft, curiosity, and small decisions that shape a life in music.

He has spent his career building singing communities, programming concerts that challenge and connect audiences, and searching for repertoire that says something worth saying. He believes that great music isn't discovered alone — it's built together, over time, with attentive ears and open minds.
