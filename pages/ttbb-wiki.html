Concerts

  • Pride As Big As Texas TCC 2026 — celebration of LGBTQ+ identity in Texas; four-section emotional arc: celebration → joy → emotional depth → empowerment
  • Women's Voices, Queer Strength TCC 2027 — celebration of women composers, divas, and legends through gay men's chorus; pop divas, Broadway, country, spirituals; four-section arc: celebration → identity → transcendence → community

Themes

  • Men's Chorus — Accessible RepertoireTTB/TB repertoire for smaller forces; musically satisfying without high demands
  • Social Justice & MemorialContemporary works addressing systemic injustice, loss, and memorial; powerful statements on equity and racial justice
  • Sacred & SpiritualDevotional and liturgical works; psalm settings, Marian texts, meditative sacred pieces
  • Dramatic & TheatricalContemporary works with theatrical approach: sound design, percussion, spoken elements, extended techniques
  • Pride & LGBTQ+ IdentityCelebration of authentic self, visibility, joy, and community; affirmation of LGBTQ+ identity
  • Religious Trauma & RecoveryHealing from faith-based harm, liberation from toxic religious ideals, reclaiming spirituality
  • Resilience & EmpowermentSurvival, resistance, social justice, standing tall in the face of adversity; vision for future
  • Patriotism & American DemocracyTTBB and dual-voicing works engaging Constitution, Declaration, Bill of Rights, and civic freedom themes
