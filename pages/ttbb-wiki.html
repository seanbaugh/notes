Concerts

- [[Pride As Big As Texas]] — TCC 2026; celebration of LGBTQ+ identity in Texas with four-section emotional arc (celebration → joy → emotional depth → empowerment)

- [[Women's Voices, Queer Strength]] — TCC 2027; celebration of women composers, divas, and legends through gay men's chorus; features pop divas, Broadway, country, spirituals; four-section arc from celebration → identity → transcendence → community

Themes

- [[Men's Chorus — Accessible Repertoire]] — TTB/TB repertoire for smaller forces; musically satisfying without high demands

- [[Social Justice & Memorial]] — contemporary works addressing systemic injustice, loss, and memorial; powerful statements on equity and racial justice

- [[Sacred & Spiritual]] — devotional and liturgical works; psalm settings, Marian texts, meditative sacred pieces with accessible writing

- [[Dramatic & Theatrical]] — contemporary works with theatrical approach: sound design, percussion, spoken elements, extended techniques

- [[Pride & LGBTQ+ Identity]] — celebration of authentic self, visibility, joy, and community; affirmation of LGBTQ+ identity

- [[Religious Trauma & Recovery]] — healing from faith-based harm, liberation from toxic religious ideals, reclaiming spirituality

- [[Resilience & Empowerment]] — survival, resistance, social justice, standing tall in face of adversity; vision for future

- [[Patriotism & American Democracy]] — TTBB and dual-voicing works engaging Constitution, Declaration, Bill of Rights, and civic freedom themes; sourced from We the People repertoire research
